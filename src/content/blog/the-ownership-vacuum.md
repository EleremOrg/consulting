---
title: "The ownership vacuum"
description: "Bad management, fuzzy goals, and future-gazing all produce motion without progress. The fix is giving people real ownership."
publishedAt: 2026-05-20
tags: ["management", "engineering-culture", "team-dynamics"]
draft: true
---

- Bad management = adding process instead of removing it. Status meetings, alignment docs, approval chains. Burns the team's best hours. Inverted: great managers remove obstacles and absorb ambiguity.
- Fuzzy goals describe activity ("ship Q3 roadmap") not outcomes ("reduce time-to-first-load by 40%"). Without outcome-defining goals, nothing is prioritizable, everyone runs in circles.
- Future-gazing feels responsible but is avoidance. "We'll need this when we scale." Most anticipated problems never materialize. Solve the concrete problem in front of you.
- Root cause: lack of true ownership. Real ownership = authority to decide + information to decide well + safety to be wrong. When someone owns a problem end-to-end they don't need process, don't need detailed goals, don't speculate about the future.
- Manager's primary job: transfer the weight of worrying about whether the thing will succeed.
